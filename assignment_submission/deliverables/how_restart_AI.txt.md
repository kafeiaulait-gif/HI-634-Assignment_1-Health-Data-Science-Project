

## Can you give me a step by step process on Chrombook to open Linux Dev environment, open terminal, and then restart AntiGravity?


No worries at all—let's take it one clear step at a time to get everything running.

<br>

Here is the exact step-by-step process to get back into Antigravity:

---

### Step 1: Open the Terminal App

1. Press the Search key (the magnifying glass icon) on your Chromebook keyboard.
2. Type Terminal into the search bar.
3. Click on the Terminal app icon that appears.

<br>

### Step 2: Open your Linux Environment

1. You will see the screen you sent a picture of earlier.
2. Click directly on the word penguin (next to the little penguin icon).
3. Wait a moment for a black command-line window to open. It might say "Starting Linux development environment" at the bottom right.

<br>

### Step 3: Type the Startup Commands

Once the window stops loading and you see your cursor blinking next to a green or white text prompt, type the following commands exactly as written, pressing Enter after each one:
1. Type this command to enter your Antigravity folder and press Enter:

```
bash
cd ~/antigravity
```


2. Type this command to launch the program and press Enter:

```
bash
./antigravity
```


Note: Remember to keep this terminal window open while you use Antigravity!
Let me know if you run into any error messages when you type those final commands, or if Antigravity launches successfully!
